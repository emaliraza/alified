About

We develop buildings with the clarity of an architect and the discipline of an engineer — places designed for the decades of life that happen inside them.

Arabian Hills Developers is the property development arm of the Arabian Star Group. Since 2009 we have delivered Grade-A offices and distinctive residences across the UAE and Pakistan, uniting land, design, construction and management under one accountable team.
