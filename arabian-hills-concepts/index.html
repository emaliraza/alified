Presenting

Each concept carries a floating badge and switcher (top-right) so you can move between them mid-meeting.

i.

Go full-screen

Press F11 (Windows) or Ctrl ⌘ F (Mac) for a clean, chrome-free presentation.

ii.

Jump with keys

Press 1, 2 or 3 to open a concept, and 0 to return to this overview.

iii.

Scroll slowly in 03

Concept 3 is a scroll-driven film. Use a mouse wheel or trackpad and pause at each chapter.

iv.

Show the mobile view

Resize the window, or append ?static=1 to Concept 3 to show its phone fallback.
