01 About the House

A Legacy Measured in Generations

Arabian Hills Developers is the property development house of the Arabian Star Group, founded on a simple conviction: that a fine building should outlast the fashions of its decade. For more than fifteen years we have acquired exceptional land, partnered with distinguished architects and delivered addresses across the UAE and Pakistan that hold their value — and their beauty — for generations. Every development carries our name, and with it our promise of quality without compromise.
